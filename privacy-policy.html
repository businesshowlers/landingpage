Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Howlers Digitals ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you visit offer.howlers.in or contact us through it. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

1. What we collect

  • Details you give us through our enquiry forms: your name, email address, mobile number, the service you are interested in and, if you picked one, the website plan.
  • Technical details sent with a form: the page you submitted it from and the time of submission.
  • Usage data collected through Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4, such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, device and browser type and approximate location. These tools use cookies or similar technologies.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and offer you a free consultation by call, WhatsApp or email.
  • To prepare a quote and, if you become a client, to deliver our services.
  • To understand how visitors use this website and to measure and improve our advertising.

By submitting a form you consent to us using your details for these purposes. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 6).

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, for example:

  • Google (Google Sheets, Gmail, Apps Script, Tag Manager and Analytics), where enquiries are stored and site usage is measured;
  • Hostinger, which hosts this website;
  • WhatsApp / Meta, if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.

These providers may store data on servers outside India. We may also disclose data if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and follow up, and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we must keep them longer by law. Client records are kept as required for accounting and legal purposes.

5. How we protect it

Your details are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in accounts with restricted access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or stop contacting you;
  • withdraw the consent you gave when submitting a form.

To make a request, email business.howlers@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

7. Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

9. Contact / Grievance

For any question or complaint about your personal data, contact us at business.howlers@gmail.com or +91 97917 18488.
